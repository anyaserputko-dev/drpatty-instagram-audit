Story series examples

Two series for patients, five frames each: a hook, the worry in plain words, how you handle it, one practical detail, one action. The wording follows your website, the photos are yours, the colors and font are your site’s.

1. “Scared of the dentist?”

For people who keep postponing treatment. It introduces IV sedation the way your website describes it and ends with a reply keyword, so the conversation starts in Direct.

Text of every frame
  1. Haven't seen a dentist in years because you're scared? You're not the only one. Here's how a visit can feel different.
  2. Often it's one bad memory The sounds, the smell, lying back and not feeling in control.
  3. IV sedation, if it's right for you Calming medication through a small IV. You stay conscious and can respond, but feel relaxed and drowsy. Patients often have little memory of the procedure, and our team tracks your vitals throughout.
  4. Bring someone to drive you home The effects can linger even after you feel fine, so plan to rest. You'll get clear instructions before and after the visit.
  5. Tell us what worries you Reply CALM and we'll walk you through your options. Or book a consultation.

2. “How much does a smile makeover cost?”

Your own #AskDrPatty question. It answers “it depends” honestly, shows what the cost depends on, mentions the financing you accept and sends people to the virtual consult.

Text of every frame
  1. How much does a smile makeover cost? The honest answer: it depends on your plan. Here's what goes into it.
  2. Every smile is a different plan The treatment, how many teeth, the materials, how complex the case is, and whether procedures are combined.
  3. A personal plan and payment options After looking at your smile and your goals, the team puts together your treatment plan and walks you through the payment options.
  4. Monthly payments are an option We accept CareCredit, Cherry, Sunbit, LendingClub and Alphaeon financing, subject to approval.
  5. Send photos for a virtual consult Upload your photos and fill out a short form. Our team reviews them and contacts you within 24 hours.

Carousel examples

Both open with a question a patient actually asks and end with one clear action.

1. Veneers, step by step

Opens with your own #AskDrPatty question and your answer, then walks through the consult, planning, materials, preparation and the first days.

Text of every frame
  1. "If I get veneers, will you take my teeth out?" Dr. Patty's answer: "Absolutely not." Here's what actually happens.
  2. The consult We check your teeth, gums and bite, and look at your whole face. Then we talk about what you want to change.
  3. The plan Digital imaging and smile planning. Shape and shade are planned to complement your natural features.
  4. Porcelain or composite Porcelain: custom-made, natural translucency, resists stains. Composite: tooth-colored resin, may be more affordable. The right choice depends on your goals, lifestyle, budget and how long you want them to last.
  5. Preparing the teeth, only if needed When teeth need preparing, local anesthesia or other comfort options can be used. Some patients qualify for minimal-prep veneers, so ask about it at your consult.
  6. Placement and the first days Once your custom veneers are ready, they're placed precisely. Mild sensitivity for a few days can happen as your mouth adjusts. If it doesn't settle, call us.
  7. Book a consultation Link in bio. Or send photos for a virtual consult: the team gets back to you within 24 hours.

2. Caring for veneers

A save-worthy carousel for patients who already have veneers, with a soft invitation to a checkup.

Text of every frame
  1. New veneers? 5 habits that help them last Save this for later.
  2. Brush twice a day, floss daily Use a soft brush and a low-abrasion toothpaste. The gums around veneers need the same care as around natural teeth.
  3. Teeth aren't tools No opening packages, biting nails or chewing ice. Veneers are strong, but they can chip.
  4. Grind at night? Ask about a guard A custom night guard helps protect your veneers and your natural teeth from grinding.
  5. Coffee, tea, red wine Porcelain veneers resist stains. A sip of water after coffee, tea or red wine helps keep any smile bright.
  6. Keep your checkups Regular cleanings and exams help catch small problems early, around veneers and everywhere else.
  7. DM us "CARE" Or book a checkup through the link in bio.
